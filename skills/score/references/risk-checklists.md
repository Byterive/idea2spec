# Risk and rules checklists by idea type

Rules change. Treat each item as a prompt to check the current official page, and record what you read. Items you did not open are RECALLED or UNVERIFIED. A hard blocker is a veto (risk = 0).

## App or game (iOS, Android)
- Store review rules on minimum functionality, copycats and saturated categories.
- In-app purchase rules: digital goods must use the store's purchase system where required.
- Kids: apps for children have extra consent, ads and data rules.
- New developer accounts may need a testing period before release on some stores.
- Data collected and the privacy policy.

## YouTube channel
- Monetization eligibility thresholds (subscribers and watch hours or Shorts views).
- Policies on reused, templated or mass-produced content and on AI-generated content.
- Restrictions for AI personas posing as health, legal, financial or political experts.
- "Made for kids" designation and its limits.
- Paid promotion and affiliate disclosure inside the video itself.

## Affiliate or content site
- Each program's terms: required disclosure sentence, where links may appear (no offline, e-book or email use unless allowed), price display rules, how fast accounts are closed when qualifying sales are missing.
- Disclosure must be clear and hard to miss, close to the link.
- Search engine policies on thin, scaled or low-value content.

## Physical product
- Product safety, labelling and certification for this class; import rules.
- Platform seller policies and fees; returns and warranty duty.
- Trademark and design-copy exposure.

## Service
- Licensing or regulated advice (legal, medical, financial).
- Contracts, liability and data handling for client data.
- Platform rules if clients come from a marketplace.

## Any type
- Privacy law touching personal data (consent, children, data export and deletion).
- Trademark and IP: names, logos, imagery, music, scraped data.
- Dependence on a single platform that can change terms or ban you.
- Required disclosure of AI-generated content where applicable.
