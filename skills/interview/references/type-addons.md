# Type add-ons

Read only the section for the chosen type. At deep depth ask one add-on question and log it by its ID (A1, A2, ...); otherwise use the topics to guide follow-ups. If an add-on answer narrows or changes an earlier answer (for example the first platform against question 1), log a Clash and ask which one wins.

## App
- A1. Which platform first (web, iOS, Android) and why?
- A2. Store rules that could block it (minimum functionality, copycat or saturated category, in-app purchase rules).
- A3. Freemium, paywall or one-off price? Who pays, and what do the closest apps charge?

## Game
- A1. The core loop in one sentence.
- A2. Session length and how often someone would return.
- A3. How will you get installs without paid ads? What does a free player give you?

## YouTube channel
- A1. Faceless or on camera? What is original in each video (templated or mass-produced videos risk demonetization)?
- A2. Income plan: ads, sponsors, affiliate links, own product. Any kids audience exposure?
- A3. How many videos can you make per week for 12 weeks?

## Affiliate or content site
- A1. Which programs will accept you, and what are their rules (disclosure sentence, link use, allowed channels)?
- A2. Where does the traffic come from, and how long until it ranks?
- A3. Where does disclosure appear on each page?

## Physical product
- A1. Unit cost quote, supplier, minimum order, shipping and returns.
- A2. Margin at the price you expect, after platform fees.
- A3. Safety, labelling or certification rules for this product class.

## Service
- A1. Price per engagement and hours needed per client.
- A2. How you find the first 5 paying clients without a platform.
- A3. Any regulated advice (legal, medical, financial)?

## Other
Ask who pays, for what, how you reach them, and the rule or platform you depend on most.
