# Question bank

Ask in rounds. "Fact" questions follow the no-pre-selected-recommendation rule; "Design" questions may carry a recommended option.

## Round 0: frame

| # | Question (header) | Kind | Feeds |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What kind of idea is it? App, game, YouTube channel, affiliate or content site, physical product, service, other (Type). Skip if the user confirmed the type in the run skill's intake phase; log it from there | Fact | lens and add-ons |
| 2 | What are the stakes? Learning or hobby, side income, full-time business, client or internal (Stakes) | Fact | rigor, length |
| 3 | Your time budget: hours per week, weeks until a first test, cash you can lose (Time budget) | Fact | feasibility, test |
| 4 | What edge do you have? Skills, existing audience, access to the customer, domain knowledge (Edge) | Fact | feasibility, differentiation |
| 5 | Search privacy: generalized only, exact wording, or no web (Privacy) | Design | research |

## Round 1: audience, evidence

| # | Question | Kind | Feeds |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | Who exactly has this problem? Name one real person or a specific segment (Who) | Fact | audience |
| 7 | What triggers the problem, and what do they do today? Which tools, workarounds or competitors do you already know? (Today) | Fact | audience, differentiation, research seed |
| 8 | What evidence do you have so far? None; opinions only; observed behaviour (searches, spending, workarounds); commitments (pre-order, pilot, deposit) (Evidence) | Fact | demand, evidence level |
| 9 | What changed recently that makes this possible or wanted? (Timing) | Fact | differentiation |

## Round 2: money, reach, stop rule

| # | Question | Kind | Feeds |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | Who pays, how and how much? User, advertiser, merchant, sponsor, client (Money) | Fact | economics |
| 11 | How will the first 100 users, viewers or visitors find it, through a channel you control? (Reach) | Fact | economics, feasibility |
| 12 | What would a 90-day win look like, and what result would make you stop? (Stop rule) | Design | cheapest test, kill criteria |

## Round 3: rules, test, scope

| # | Question | Kind | Feeds |
|---|---|---|---|
| 13 | Any exposure to kids, health, finance or legal advice, personal data, trademarks or IP, AI-generated content, or dependence on one platform's rules? (Rules) | Fact | risk |
| 14 | Which cheap test will you actually run, and by when? Offer options for the chosen type (Test) | Design | cheapest test |
| 15 | What is explicitly out of scope, and what constraints are fixed (platform, stack, budget, tools)? (Scope) | Design | spec |

## Clarify questions
After scoring, clarify questions use this shape, at most 3 per round: the dimension, why it blocks the verdict, and the cheapest evidence that would answer it. Same answer options as fact questions.
