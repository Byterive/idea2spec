# Research plan format

Show this to the user before searching.

```
Privacy choice: generalized | exact wording | no web
Idea as I will search it: <one generalized sentence>
Facets and example searches:
1. Alternatives: "<category> alternatives", "best <category> for <audience>"
2. Monetization: "<category> pricing", "how <category> makes money"
3. Demand: "<category> reviews", "<category> reddit", store ratings
4. Complaints: "<category> problems", "<competitor> shutdown", "<category> failed"
Cost line (economics needs it): searches for what it costs to run this idea, such as AI or API fees per user, hosting, app-store or payment fees, and a rival's published unit costs. If the interview gave no cost figure, ask for one number in the plan, or write UNKNOWN and make it a measurement in the cheapest test.
Domains I expect to read: official sites, app stores, review sites, forums, platform policy pages
Call budget: <n> per facet, <total> overall
Not searched: <names, mechanics, personal details kept out of queries>
```

Ask: "Search this? Say yes, or change it."

Notes:
- For a YouTube channel: other channels in the niche, their upload rate and sponsors, the platform's monetization and reused-content rules.
- For an app or game: store listings, ratings counts, pricing, platform review rules.
- For an affiliate or content site: programs' terms, disclosure rules, who ranks for the main keywords.
- For a service or product: competitor price pages, supplier or marketplace fees, regulation.
- Reliable benchmark numbers are scarce and often conflict. Treat them as dated starting priors, never as proof. Record each in the ledger's Benchmarks table with its definition and denominator. If none is found after the call budget, record it in Gaps and do not repeat the search; the score skill then caps economics at 1.
